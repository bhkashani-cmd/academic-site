Dr. Behzad Hassannezhad Kashani

Researcher in Media Management and Communication Studies

Dr. Behzad Hassannezhad Kashani is a researcher in Media Management with academic interests in media policy, cyberspace policymaking, communication studies, marketing communication, and strategic media planning.

## Research Interests
Media Management
Research on media organizations, management approaches, and future developments in media systems.

Media Policy and Cyberspace Governance
Research on internet management, cyberspace policymaking, policy processes, and governance issues.

Communication Studies
Research interests include human communication, communication technologies, and media-related issues.

Marketing Communication
Research on advertising planning, strategic media planning, and communication strategies.

Management Studies
Research activities related to organizational and management topics.
