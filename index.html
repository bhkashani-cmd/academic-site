Dr. Behzad Hassannezhad Kashani

Researcher in Media Management and Communication Studies

Dr. Behzad Hassannezhad Kashani is a researcher in Media Management with research interests in media policy, cyberspace policymaking, communication studies, marketing communication, and strategic media planning.

## Research Interests
Media Management
Research on media organizations, media systems, and management approaches in the field of communication.

Media Policy and Cyberspace Policymaking
Research on internet management, cyberspace policymaking processes, and policy-related issues in digital environments.

Communication Studies
Research interests include human communication, communication technologies, and media-related social and organizational issues.

Marketing Communication
Research on advertising planning, strategic media planning, and communication strategies.

Management Studies
Research activities related to management concepts and organizational issues.
