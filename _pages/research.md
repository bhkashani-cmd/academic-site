# Research

Dr. Behzad Hassannezhad Kashani's research interests include interdisciplinary areas in economics, management, accounting, and scholarly communication.

## Research Areas

- Economics and Applied Economic Analysis
- Management and Organizational Studies
- Accounting and Financial Research
- Scholarly Publishing and Journal Management
- Bibliometrics and Scientometrics
- Research Evaluation and Impact Assessment
- Open Science and Publication Ethics

## Current Research Interests

Current research activities focus on improving the quality, visibility, and impact of academic research through:

- Research evaluation methodologies
- Scholarly communication and publishing practices
- Bibliometric analysis of scientific literature
- Editorial processes and journal development
- Academic knowledge dissemination

## Research Approach

My research approach combines quantitative and qualitative methods, with particular interest in evidence-based analysis, systematic literature reviews, bibliometric techniques, and the advancement of responsible research practices.
