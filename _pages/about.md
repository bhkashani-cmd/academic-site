# Dr. Behzad Hassannezhad Kashani

Dr. Behzad Hassannezhad Kashani is a researcher, academic editor, and university lecturer with research interests in economics, management, accounting, scholarly publishing, bibliometrics, research evaluation, and public policy.

He is the **License Holder and Director-in-Charge of the International Journal of Management, Accounting and Economics (IJMAE)**, an academic journal licensed by the Ministry of Culture and Islamic Guidance of Iran. Since 2014, he has been involved in journal management, editorial processes, scholarly communication, and research dissemination. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

His academic activities include research, university teaching, editorial management, peer review, and contribution to the development of scientific communication. He has served as a visiting lecturer at several higher education institutions, including Imam Reza International University, Baqir al-Olum University, University of Applied Science and Technology, Kavian Higher Education Institute, and Islamic Azad University. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
