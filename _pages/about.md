# Dr. Behzad Hassannezhad Kashani

Dr. Behzad Hassannezhad Kashani is a researcher and academic in the fields of Media Management, Communication, Internet Policy, and Marketing. He holds a Ph.D. in Media Management (By Research) from Imam Reza International University, where his doctoral research focused on developing a desirable system for cyberspace policymaking in Iran. :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}

He also holds an M.A. in Business Management (Marketing) and a B.Sc. in Insurance Management. His research interests include Media Policy, Media Future Studies, Internet Management, Human Communication, Marketing Communication, Advertising Planning, and Strategic Media Planning. :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}

Dr. Hassannezhad Kashani is the License Holder and Director-in-Charge of the *International Journal of Management, Accounting, and Economics (IJMAE)* since 2014. He has also served as a visiting lecturer at Imam Reza International University, Baqir al-Olum University, University of Applied Science and Technology, Kavian Higher Education Institute, and Islamic Azad University. :contentReference[oaicite:11]{index=11}

His academic contributions include research articles published in international journals and conference presentations on topics related to cyberspace policymaking, communication technologies, marketing, customer behavior, insurance services, and knowledge management. :contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}

He has received academic recognition, including an Outstanding Dissertation Award for his doctoral research on cyberspace policymaking and an Outstanding Research Award for research presented at the First International Conference of Mobile Value Added Services at Sharif University of Technology. :contentReference[oaicite:13]{index=13}
